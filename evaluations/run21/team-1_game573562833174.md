**Distinct viable strategies?** One for P1 (rush a line); **none viable for P2** (cut reclaimed, race lost by tempo). This is the thinnest equilibrium in the slate.
**Counter-play.** **Absent.** The defender's natural tool (the cut) is neutralized by custodian reclaim. Worse than R8, where the cut beat the rush.
**Short-term vs long-term.** Connection-win forces global path planning (a plus over packing), but the rush resolves it in ~17 plies with no contest.
**Emergent concepts observed.** **Cut-reclaim cascades** (one stone flips a multi-stone run) are genuinely emergent and surprising — the one interesting thing here. But they resolve *one-sidedly* in the attacker's favor, so they add spectacle, not balance.
**Does the grid substrate matter?** It's the R8-replay concern made concrete: **9×9 flat, 4-neighbor, no bridges** is in the small-board class where connection lacks room for ladders/feints. P1's straight line can't be routed around because reclaim defeats cuts; P2 has no room to build a counter-threat.
**Does the propagation kernel matter?** It's **off** — and that absence is why PPO can't learn it: connection-win gives reward only at completion, and with no influence field there is no dense gradient. GE~0 is a training artifact of sparse reward, not (only) a depth verdict.
**Capture contribution.** Custodian is *the* live mechanic, but it **breaks the game**: by making cuts reclaimable it removes the defender's counter-strategy and produces the 0.50 rush bias.
**First-mover advantage / seat balance.** **Structurally broken.** Perpendicular axes + first-move tempo + reclaim-defeats-cut → bias 0.50 at every komi; pie can't fix it.

**G6 verdict (the point of this game).** **G6 FAILS — on two independent counts.**
1. *Untrainable:* connection-win + propagation-none = sparse reward, no gradient → GE~0 (the 3rd confirmation).
2. *The design itself is rush-broken and is **worse than R8**.* R8 (surround/Go-liberty capture) had a working defense — a single cut forced P1 to detour and let P2 win, giving genuine cut-vs-race depth and rough balance. Swapping in **custodian** capture lets the attacker **reclaim** every cut, deleting the defender's only tool and collapsing the game to a determined perpendicular rush. "Restore connection-win" does **not** restore R8's depth; the custodian/connection combo is a regression. To revive R8 you need R8's **surround** capture (cuts that *can't* be reclaimed) **plus** a larger board (room for ladders/feints) **plus** influence-in-win-logic (a dense gradient PPO can learn) — exactly the R8-replay/PIVOT prescription, not custodian on 9×9.
