### Strategy guides
**P1 (horizontal connect):** Build a contiguous row (capture-proof), grab the central crossing early, anticipate which row P2's column will cross. Never leave a flankable gap stone.
**P2 (vertical connect):** Do NOT mirror. Contest the crossing cell of P1's likely row; use custodian cuts to sever any gappy P1 chain; build your own column contiguously. The first-move disadvantage is real and uncompensated — claim the intersection to fight back.

- Address PPO trainability (e.g., convolutional/graph policy with spatial bias, AlphaZero-style search) — the design's depth is real; the obstruction is that the project's flat-MLP PPO cannot learn connection-win.
